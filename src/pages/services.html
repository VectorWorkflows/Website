?Questions

Before you ask.

Do my customers need to create an account?
No. Orders and booking requests arrive from the customer's own WhatsApp number, so you always know who is asking. Requests stay "pending" until you confirm them, and anyone wasting your time can be blocked in one tap. For high-value slots you can ask for a small UPI token amount.
What happens after the first year?
The care plan keeps hosting, bot hosting and small changes going, at {{ pricing.care_monthly }} a month or {{ pricing.care_yearly }} a year. If you'd rather not continue, everything that was built stays yours to run wherever you like.
Can I change my prices or hours myself?
Yes. Your Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business details are yours to edit whenever you like. For the website and the bots, send the change on WhatsApp: text, prices, hours and photos count as small changes, included in year one and on the care plan after that.
What do you need from me to start?
Your business name, what you offer and what you charge, your opening hours, a few photos, and access to whatever the service runs on: your Google account, your WhatsApp number or your phone line. The build time starts once those are in.
What if I'm not sure which ones I need?
Choose "Not sure — help me choose" on the start page, or book a 15-minute call. Describe how your week runs and you'll get a straight answer on what's worth setting up.
