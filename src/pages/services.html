iHow pricing works

Starting prices, not surprises.

What "from" means
The price covers the scope listed under Included. If your job needs more than that, you get a quote for the extra before any work starts, never after.
Provider costs
Voice and messaging systems run on third-party telephony and messaging providers. Their usage is billed at cost, with no markup.
Build windows
Each window starts once the scope is agreed and the accounts it needs (phone number, WhatsApp Business, Google access) are in place.
