01Where admissions go

Admission season, hour by hour.

  1. 11:00

    The same question, forty times

    Batch timings, fees, boards, holidays. The same questions arrive again and again, on WhatsApp, by phone and at the door, and each one takes a teacher away from something else.

  2. 17:30

    Teaching when the phone rings

    Classes run in the evening, which is exactly when parents get home and call. Every teacher is in a classroom.

  3. 21:45

    The question asked after hours

    A parent asks about batch timings at night and hears back the next afternoon. By then, a centre down the road has already replied.

  4. Day 3

    The demo class nobody followed up

    A child sat in on a free class. Nobody got back to the parent afterwards, and they took the silence to mean the centre wasn't keen.

02The arithmetic

?Questions

Questions centres ask.

Can the site be in English and Hindi?
Yes. The Reliance Coaching Centre site is built in both, with a switch at the top and the Hindi set in a typeface made for it. Other languages can work the same way.
Can parents see the fees without calling?
If you want them to. Fees can be a full table, a "from" price or a clear "ask us". It's one setting, and you can change your mind later.
What will the WhatsApp bot answer?
The questions you give it answers to: batches, timings, fees, boards, the address, holidays. Anything else it passes to you, and it says so to the parent.
Will it book demo classes by itself?
It takes the request and passes it to you to confirm. You decide which batch a child sits in, and when.
What about results and toppers?
Only results you can stand behind, and we don't put children's names or photographs on a website. A site of confirmed facts reads as more serious than a collage.
