01Where members go

From first message to last visit.

  1. 23:10

    The 11 pm decision

    Someone finally decides to start, messages to ask about a trial, and hears back the next afternoon. By then the moment has passed.

  2. 06:00

    The trial that didn't show

    Booked on Monday for Thursday at six. No reminder, a cold morning, and the place in the class goes empty.

  3. 09:30

    The class that went well

    They loved it and went home. Nobody asked for a review while it was fresh, and a week later nobody will think to.

  4. Week 3

    The member who drifted

    Three visits a week, then one, then none. Nobody noticed until the cancellation email arrived.

04Members who drift

Notice the quiet ones.

  1. 01

    A list by last visit

    The repeat-customer tracker keeps members in order of when they last came in, so the drifting ones rise to the top on their own.

  2. 02

    A friendly message, in your words

    Past the gap you choose, a member gets one WhatsApp from your business number: a hello, not a sales push. You write it once.

  3. 03

    Only to people who said yes

    Members agree to WhatsApp messages when they join, as WhatsApp's rules require, and anyone can ask you to stop.

?Questions

Questions studios ask.

Can people book regular classes, not just trials?
Yes. The booking link can list any class or session you run, and the reminders work the same way for all of them.
We already have gym software. Do we need to switch?
No. If your software already takes bookings, the bot can send people straight to it, and we'll talk through whether reminders should come from it or from us before anything is built.
Won't the nudge annoy members?
It's one message in your words, sent only after the gap you choose, to members who agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. Anyone can ask you to stop.
What does the bot say when it doesn't know?
That it's passing the question to the team, and then it does. You'll see the chat on your phone like any other.
