?Questions

Questions detailers ask.

Can the site show my real prices?
Yes, once you've confirmed them. Until then a service reads "Quote" or "Priced on inspection". A price on your site is a promise to a customer, so nothing goes up that you haven't signed off.
Do I need the 3D car?
No. The 3D car is the flagship's showpiece, loaded only on devices that can run it, with a still image everywhere else. Your site can lead with your own photographs instead and still have the coverage switcher and the tint previewer.
How does the tint previewer deal with the law?
It flags any front-window shade darker than your state allows. The AZ Detailers preview is set to Arizona, where film on the front side windows must let through at least 33% of the light. We set it to the figure for your state, which you confirm.
Where do booking requests go?
To you, by email, text or WhatsApp, whichever you check. Each one names the vehicle, the services and the day the customer would like. You call or text back to confirm.
What happens when I miss a call?
With the missed-call responder, the caller gets a text within seconds asking about the car, you get an alert with their number, and the call is logged in a sheet that updates when they reply. You can call our demo line to try it.
