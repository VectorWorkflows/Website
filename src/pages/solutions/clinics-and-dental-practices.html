01Where patients go

A day at the front desk.

  1. 08:50

    The empty chair at nine

    A patient who booked three weeks ago has forgotten. A reminder the evening before would have kept the slot, or freed it in time for someone else.

  2. 09:05

    Two lines, one receptionist

    A patient at the desk, another on hold, a third call ringing out. The third caller wanted a new-patient appointment, and by lunch has booked somewhere else.

  3. 12:40

    The voicemail nobody plays back

    A message left at lunchtime gets heard at five. Plenty of callers won't leave one at all; they'll just try the next practice on the map.

  4. 18:30

    The check-up nobody booked

    Recalls slip because nobody has time to work through the list. The patient isn't unhappy. They just weren't asked to come back.

04Privacy

What stays out of the messages.

A reminder says who it's from, the day and the time, and how to change it. Nothing about the treatment, and nothing a patient would mind someone reading over their shoulder.

None of this needs your patient records. It works from an appointment time and a phone number, and your practice software stays exactly where it is.

Tell us which privacy rules your practice works under (HIPAA in the US, for example) before anything is set up, and we'll keep identifying details out of messages and sheets accordingly.

?Questions

Questions practices ask.

Will messages mention a patient's treatment?
No. Reminders carry the practice's name, the day and time, and how to change it. That's all they need.
Does this replace our practice software?
No. It runs beside it. The booking link can be one your software already gives you, or a simple one we set up, and appointments land in a calendar you already use.
What about patients who don't use WhatsApp?
The reply to a missed call goes out as a text message, so it reaches any mobile phone. Reminders go out on WhatsApp; patients without it can still book online or be called.
Can patients reply to a reminder?
Yes. A reply arrives on your WhatsApp like any other message, so the front desk can move the appointment there and then.
