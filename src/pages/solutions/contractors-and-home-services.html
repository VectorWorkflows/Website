?Questions

Questions trades ask.

Can I keep my current number?
Usually, yes. Calls you don't answer on your existing number can be forwarded to the line the system watches, so customers keep calling the number they know. We check with your phone provider before anything is set up.
What if the caller rang from a landline?
A text can't reach a landline. You still get the alert with their number, so you can ring them back.
What if it's an emergency?
The text is written in your words, so it can tell people what to do if it's urgent. The system makes sure they hear back; it doesn't replace you picking up.
How does the site log work?
Your crew send a photo and a short note to one WhatsApp number. Each becomes a dated row in a sheet, with the photo attached and the location taken from the message. See the case study.
Is there anything to register for texting in the US?
Yes. Businesses texting from a US local number have to register with the carriers (it's called A2P 10DLC). It's done as part of setting up the line, which is one reason phone services say "plus phone-line activation time".
