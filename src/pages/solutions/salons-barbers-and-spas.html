01Where clients go

Where the chair goes empty.

  1. 10:15

    Hands in someone's hair

    The phone rings in the middle of a colour. By the time you've rinsed, the caller has booked somewhere that answered.

  2. 14:00

    The no-show at two

    A cut booked a fortnight ago, forgotten. A reminder the evening before keeps the chair full, or frees it in time for someone else.

  3. 18:40

    A regular, booked through an app

    Someone who has sat in your chair for years books through a marketplace app, and depending on your plan, the app takes a share of a booking you'd have had anyway.

  4. Week 7

    The regular who went quiet

    Every six weeks like clockwork, until they weren't. A short message saying it's been a while costs nothing to send.

?Questions

Questions salons ask.

Should I leave the booking apps?
Not necessarily. They're good at finding new clients. The point of your own link is that the clients who already know you don't have to book through someone else.
Where do bookings go?
Into your own calendar. The client gets a WhatsApp reminder the day before, from your business number.
Can clients move an appointment?
They reply to the reminder and you move it, or they rebook with the same link.
Will review requests put clients off?
It's one message after the visit, at a time you choose, with your Google review link in it. Nothing follows if they don't reply.
Who gets the overdue nudge?
Clients who agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp and haven't been in for longer than the gap you set. One message, in your words; anyone can ask you to stop.
