WhatsApp Business setup or a WhatsApp auto-reply bot: an owner's guide

Two different things share the name WhatsApp Business. One is a free app you can set up properly in an afternoon; the other lets a bot answer for you at any hour. Here's what each does, what Meta charges for, and how to tell which one your business needs.

Two things with one name

The WhatsApp Business app is a free app on your phone, separate from personal WhatsApp. You answer customers yourself, with some tools that make it quicker.

The WhatsApp Business Platform is how software talks to WhatsApp. It's what a bot runs on: your number is connected to a system that can read a message, look up the answer in your own list, reply, and pass the chat to a person when it should.

What the app does well

Set up properly, the free app already does a lot. This is what our WhatsApp Business setup covers:

  • A catalogue of what you sell or offer, with prices and photos, that customers can browse inside the chat.
  • Greeting and away messages, so a first message or one sent after hours gets an answer.
  • Quick replies: your common answers saved as shortcuts, typed in two keystrokes.
  • Labels to sort chats into new, waiting for payment, done, or whatever fits your week.
  • A click-to-chat link and a QR code for your website, your counter and your business card.

What the app can't do is think. Its away message is the same text for everyone. It can't look up whether Saturday is free, take an order with the details in the right place, or decide that a question needs you. A person still answers every chat.

What a bot adds

  • Answers from your own list: prices, hours, location, the questions you're asked every day, at any hour.
  • Booking and order requests, taken in a structured way and passed to you to confirm. It doesn't confirm anything you haven't agreed.
  • A handover to a person when the question is new, unusual or needs judgement, and it tells the customer that's what it's doing.
  • A record: with the enquiry tracker, every request lands in one sheet with its status.

What Meta charges for

The app is free. On the Platform, Meta charges for some messages and not others, and the line is simple once you see it:

  • Replies are free. When a customer messages you, you can reply freely for the next 24 hours. A bot answering questions sits almost entirely here.
  • Messages your business starts are charged. A reminder, a follow-up or a nudge sent outside that 24-hour window has to use a template Meta has approved, and since July 2025 Meta charges for those per message. The price depends on the kind of message and the customer's country.
  • Some are free in the window. Utility templates, such as an appointment confirmation, cost nothing when sent within those 24 hours.

Can I keep the app on my phone?

Often, yes. Meta now lets one number work in the Business app and on the Platform at the same time, which it calls coexistence: a bot handles the routine chats, and you can still open the app and reply yourself. It needs a recent version of the app and isn't offered for every number or country, so we check yours before we start. Where it isn't available, the bot runs on a number of its own.

A rough rule: if you answer the same questions more than a few times a day, or a lot of your messages arrive when you're closed, the bot earns its place. If not, a properly set-up app is enough, and cheaper.

The time it takes now

Count a normal week of chats and how long each takes you. Most of the saving comes from the questions you've answered a hundred times before.

Common questions.

Will customers know they're talking to a bot?
They should. We recommend the first reply says it's automatic: people are fine with a fast, accurate answer from a bot, and what annoys them is a bot pretending to be a person.
Does Meta have to approve anything?
Yes. Meta reviews the business's display name, approves the templates used for messages you start, and may ask to verify the business. We set it up and walk you through it; it's why WhatsApp services say "plus Meta's approval time".
Can the bot take payments?
Not ours. It takes the request and passes it to you; you take payment the way you do now.
Can I message all my customers at once?
Only those who agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp, and each message you start uses an approved template and is charged by Meta. Done carelessly, it gets numbers reported and limited.
