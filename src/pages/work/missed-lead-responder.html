01The problem

A missed call is a lost customer.

For most local and service businesses, an unanswered phone is one of the most expensive gaps in the whole operation. A caller who doesn't leave a voicemail has usually just tried the next business on the list.

By the time anyone notices the missed call sitting in the log, the window to win that customer back has usually closed. The fix isn't answering every call. It's making sure nobody who calls is left without a reply.

03How it works

Missed call in. Structured lead out.

  1. 01

    The call goes unanswered

    The business line runs through Twilio. The moment a call ends without being picked up, a webhook fires to the FastAPI service.

  2. 02

    The caller gets a reply within seconds

    An SMS goes back automatically: it acknowledges the call, answers the obvious first question, and invites a reply.

  3. 03

    The owner is alerted on Telegram

    A bot message arrives with the caller's number and two buttons, Call back and Mark handled, so it can be dealt with from anywhere.

  4. 04

    A lead row lands in Google Sheets

    Number, time and status are written to the business's own sheet, so there's always a live list of who called and where each one stands.

  5. 05

    Replies update the row

    When the caller texts back, the row changes to Replied with their reason, and the owner is pinged again.

  6. 06

    Unhandled leads come back round

    If a lead is still open, a reminder fires, so nothing drifts between "the phone rang" and "someone followed up."

04Stack

What it runs on

Twilio
The phone line: missed-call webhooks, outbound and inbound SMS
FastAPI
The service that receives every event and decides what happens next
MongoDB
Lead state: who called, what's been sent, what's still open
Telegram Bot API
Owner alerts with inline Call back and Mark handled actions
Google Sheets
The lead list the business actually looks at

What it doesn't do

It doesn't answer the call or hold a live conversation. It gets the first reply out fast and makes sure a person follows up.
