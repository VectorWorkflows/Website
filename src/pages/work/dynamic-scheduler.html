01The problem

Every day doesn't start the same way.

Most calendars assume you'll wake at the same time and follow the same plan every day. Wake up late once and the whole day's schedule is wrong before you've had coffee.

Re-editing every event by hand is its own chore, so most people just stop bothering. This bot is built around one idea: your day looks different depending on when it starts.

03How it works

Set templates once, adapt every day.

  1. 01

    Sign in with Google

    Connect your calendar once: a standard Google consent screen asks you to allow access to your calendar events.

  2. 02

    Define three versions of your day

    Build a schedule for a date by hand and save it as your early, normal or late template, or start from the defaults.

  3. 03

    Send /awake

    The bot checks the time you sent it and applies the matching template to today's calendar.

  4. 04

    Adjust with short commands

    Clear Day empties a date. Add Task drops a task into the day's plan.

  5. 05

    Plan ahead

    Prepare a future date by entering the time you expect to wake up, or by adding tasks to it in advance.

04Stack

What it runs on

Telegram Bot API
The whole interface: /awake, Clear Day, Add Task and the replies
Python
The scheduling logic: reading the time, choosing and applying templates
Google Calendar
Where the day's schedule is actually built

What it doesn't do

It isn't a general natural-language calendar assistant and doesn't try to understand arbitrary requests. It does one job: fitting your day to when it actually started, using templates you define.
