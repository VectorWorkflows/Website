Privacy & Data Transparency

When you use Vector Workflows, you trust us to manage your calendar and automate your daily routines. We take that responsibility seriously.

We build our tools with a simple rule: your data belongs to you, and you stay in full control. This page explains clearly what information our Dynamic Circadian Calendar Scheduler interacts with, how it works under the hood, and how you can delete your data at any time.

What Information We Process

To generate dynamic schedules directly on your calendar, our Telegram bot processes only the absolute minimum data required:

  • Google Calendar Access: Secure OAuth authorization tokens that allow us to create, update, and clear specific circadian schedule entries on your Google Calendar.
  • Telegram Identifier & Timestamps: Your Telegram User ID and the exact timestamps of the messages or wake-up pings you send to the bot.

We do not request access to your contacts, emails, location, or any Google services outside of your Google Calendar.

How Your Data Is Used

Every piece of data processed has a single, functional purpose:

  • Pre-Configuring Placeholder Templates: Upon initial setup, the app prepares schedule blocks roughly two months ahead of today's date. This ensures your near-term calendar stays clean and unobstructed until you actively trigger a daily routine.
  • Real-Time Circadian Scheduling: When you send a wake-up ping to the bot in Telegram, the system evaluates your input and writes your customized, buffered daily schedule directly to your Google Calendar, starting within 15 minutes of your ping.
  • Session Management: Storing your encrypted access tokens so the bot can write updates without forcing you to re-authenticate every time.

Our Google Data Guarantees (Limited Use)

Our application strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements:

  • Zero Advertising: Your calendar data is never used to serve advertisements, build ad profiles, or target marketing.
  • Zero Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your Google Calendar data to any third party.
  • Zero AI Model Training: Your personal schedule and calendar entries are never fed into AI or machine learning models.
  • Zero Human Inspection: No member of the Vector Workflows team reads your calendar events unless you explicitly grant written permission for technical troubleshooting or safety reasons.

You Stay in Control (Instant Deletion)

You can disconnect your account and wipe your data from our system whenever you choose:

  • In-Bot One-Click Wipe: Tap the Delete and Logout button inside the Telegram bot interface. This immediately purges your stored Google tokens and session credentials from our servers.
  • Google Security Settings: You can also revoke access directly from your Google Security Dashboard at any time.

Security & Encryption

All communication between Telegram, our servers, and Google’s APIs is protected using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit. Your secret tokens are stored securely in isolated environment variables and encrypted datastores.
