Clause 01 — Collection

What Information
We Process

To generate dynamic schedules directly on your calendar, our Telegram bot processes only the absolute minimum data required — nothing more.

D.01 — CALENDAR

Google Calendar Access

Secure OAuth authorization tokens that let us create, update, and clear specific circadian schedule entries on your Google Calendar.

D.02 — TELEGRAM

Telegram ID & Timestamps

Your Telegram User ID and the exact timestamps of the messages or wake-up pings you send to the bot.

We do not request access to your contacts, emails, location, or any Google services outside of your Google Calendar.

Clause 02 — Usage

How Your Data
Is Used

Every piece of data processed has a single, functional purpose.

01

Pre-Configuring Placeholder Templates

Upon initial setup, the app prepares schedule blocks roughly two months ahead of today's date. This keeps your near-term calendar clean and unobstructed until you actively trigger a daily routine.

02

Real-Time Circadian Scheduling

When you send a wake-up ping to the bot in Telegram, the system evaluates your input and writes your customized, buffered daily schedule directly to your Google Calendar, starting within 15 minutes of your ping.

03

Session Management

Storing your encrypted access tokens so the bot can write updates without forcing you to re-authenticate every time.

Clause 04 — Deletion

You Stay
In Control

You can disconnect your account and wipe your data from our system whenever you choose.

C.01 — IN-BOT

One-Click Wipe

Tap the Delete and Logout button inside the Telegram bot interface. This immediately purges your stored Google tokens and session credentials from our servers.

C.02 — GOOGLE

Security Settings

You can also revoke access directly from your Google Security Dashboard at any time.

Clause 05 — Security

Encryption

All communication between Telegram, our servers, and Google's APIs is protected using industry-standard encryption in transit. Your secret tokens are stored in isolated environment variables and encrypted datastores.
